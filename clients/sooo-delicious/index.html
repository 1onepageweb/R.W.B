Wait… what’s a Waffle Burger?

A juicy seasoned burger — bun and all — pressed between two scorching-hot waffle irons, so the whole thing cooks to a golden crisp and locks in the melted cheese and toppings. You’ve had burgers. You haven’t had this.

The Big Five

Every meal comes with our signature sides energy. Pick your number, call it in.

1

Chicken & Waffle Combo

Crispy fried chicken over our signature Belgian cornbread waffle. Medium: one side + half waffle. Large: two sides + a whole waffle.

Waffle: Belgian Cornbread · Caramel Apple · Carnival Confetti · Lemoncello Cream
Protein: wings · tenders · steak · fried cod

2

Mac Cracken Bowls

A hearty bowl of creamy mac & cheese loaded your way.

Buffalo Mac — crispy buffalo chicken, ranch drizzle
Philly Cheesesteak Mac — tender steak, peppers, onions, melted cheese
Fish & Chips Mac — crispy fried fish & golden chips

3

Build-Your-Own Waffle Burger

One patty or two, pressed golden on the waffle iron and stacked with cheese and toppings.

Toppings: lettuce · pickles · onions · peppers · jalapeños · American cheese · bacon
Sauces: ketchup · mayo · mustard · BBQ · gold · ranch & more

4

Chicken Tuxedo

Fried chicken stacked in a bun and pressed to perfection on the waffle iron, served with our signature sides. The dressed-up classic.

5

Philly Cheesesteak

Tender Philly-style steak with peppers, onions & melted cheese on a fresh roll, served with crispy seasoned fries.
